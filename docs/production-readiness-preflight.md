## 7. Notifications, Reminders & Cron Audit

- **Delivery Truthfulness:**
  - Status mapping: When Resend responds with HTTP 200, the job status is marked `accepted` (`"Accepted by provider, delivery unconfirmed"`).
  - It is **never** marked `delivered`.
  - When `RESEND_API_KEY` is omitted, jobs are marked `simulated` (`"Dry-run (not sent)"`).
- **Atomic Queue Processing:**
  - Jobs are claimed via conditional `UPDATE notification_jobs SET status = 'sending', locked_until = ... WHERE status IN ('queued', 'retry_scheduled')`.
  - Stuck jobs with expired leases are reclaimed automatically.
- **Timing & Boundary Rules:**
  - 24-hour and 2-hour reminder rules.
  - Quiet hours (22:00–08:00) shift reminders to 08:00 next morning in business timezone.
  - Lateness threshold: reminders delayed $> 15$ minutes are dropped (`skipped_late`).
  - Superseding: If booking start time or version changes, previous scheduled jobs are marked `superseded`.
  - Customer Consent: Customers with `consent_email_reminders = 0`, `opted_out_at_utc != null`, or `pending_deletion = 1` are marked `skipped_consent`.
- **Manual WhatsApp:** No automated WhatsApp bot or message delivery claims; generates manual `https://wa.me/<phone>?text=...` links for client-side sending.
